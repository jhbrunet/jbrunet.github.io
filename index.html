02 // Methodology

How Speed Is Built

No rigid dogma—only physics, biomechanics, and what moves an athlete faster down the fall line.

01 // LINE & FORCE

Trajectory Geometry

Apex-driven line geometry paired with shin-to-boot leverage, hip projection, and clean pressure release between turns.

02 // ON-SNOW PROOF

Full-Pace Demonstration

Every technical cue is tested on my own skis at race speed—reading the exact ruts, surface chatter, and boilerplate ice my athletes ski.

03 // INDIVIDUALIZATION

Anthropometric Calibration

Adapting stance width, tactical risk profiles, and dryland conditioning to each racer's lever ratios, anatomy, and competitive mindset.

04 // VIDEO TELEMETRY

Frame-by-Frame Breakdown

Distilling high-frame-rate footage into one concrete, actionable adjustment per gate sequence right on the hill.
